French people hate potatoes, or at least used to. In the 1780s, the French were dying of starvation. It wasn't like there wasn't anything to eat though, since there were plenty of potatoes around. But, the French rather starve than eating a nutritious potato. For hundreds of years, they genuinely believed potatoes caused deadly illnesses, to the point the French parliament even officially banned the cultivation of them. One guy, commissioned by the king, who knew potatoes were actually a superfood, was asked to change that. But, how do you convince the working class to eat what they think is poisonous pig food? Simple. He bought a two-acre land, planted a bunch of potatoes, and then paid a armed guard to protect the farm at all cost. People assumed armed guards meant something very valuable was growing there, so they began to steal the potatoes, causing them to become popular. The farmer even told his guards to accept bribes and to not actually catch anyone trying to steal something. The reverse psychology worked amazing, and then the French Revolution started.

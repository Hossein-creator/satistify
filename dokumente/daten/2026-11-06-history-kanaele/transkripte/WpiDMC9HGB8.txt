What if Germany won World War II? Well, to answer the what, we got to answer the how. Hitler made notoriously three massive blunders: attacking the Soviets in the winter, letting Britain breathe after Dunkirk, and declaring war on the US for literally no reason. So, let's flip that. Germany invades the USSR in the summer, finishes them off, blitzes Britain before they regroup, and makes peace with the US. It's wild, but honestly, doable. Ireland was neutral, so the Nazis could have hopped right into mainland Britain if they kept the pace and didn't have to worry about the Irish. And don't forget, early in the war, much of the US public was weirdly pro-German. If the Nazis hadn't opened the gas chambers, maybe the sympathy would have lasted. Okay, so now the Reich grows from this to this. What now? Well, instead of fantasizing, let's look at the past massive empires. Remember the Roman Empire? Too big to hold. Too many people forced under one flag. You can't slap an eagle on everyone's passport and expect harmony. So, best-case scenario, the German Empire splits in half. An Eastern Reich, a Western Reich. One thrives, one will eventually rot. Same old imperial cycle. Eventually, the rotten Reich falls apart. Groups come together to set up countries again, and they start to harass the other Reich till that one too loses its Lebensraum. And in the end, Germany is right back where it started. Oh, well, time for World War III.

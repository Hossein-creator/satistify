Have you ever wondered just how dirty medieval cities were? Well, you can see for yourself here in this narrow medieval alleyway. The Blindel Aelstra or Line Donkey Street is a street at the heart of the historic center of Bruge in Belgium. Look to the side and you'll spot a black square left untouched after restorations in the 1990s. Proof of how dirty the city hall's facade got and traffic passed through this narrow street for decades. Today, no more cars, just pedestrians and cyclists using this charming medieval alleyway to get from the Bruge Square to the fish market. But why Blind Donkey Street? Back in the 1490s, there was an inn called the Blender Aeler. The name likely comes from a legend about a blindfolded donkey carrying goods through the city. However, it could also come from when brewers would blindfold their donkeys so they wouldn't get dizzy walking in circles on the brewery mill. No matter which story you believe, one thing's for sure, this little street is full of history. So if you ever find yourself in Bruge, don't just walk through.

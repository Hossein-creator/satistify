Worst dads in history. What kind of male role model shaped the 20th century's most infamous dictator? Well, unsurprisingly, Adolf Hitler's dad, Alois, was not an ideal parent. Orphaned young with a somewhat murky parentage, Alois Schicklgruber would ultimately take the last name Hitler of the relatives who raised him. This rocky childhood produced a deeply flawed man. Alois was known to have affairs, including with a 16-year-old maid named Klara, who would ultimately be his second wife and baby Adolf's mother. She may also have been his cousin, and they called each other uncle and niece as pet names. Eesh. While Klara seems to have been a fine person, Alois was a domineering parent and verbally and physically abusive, mostly to Klara. However, things spiraled when Alois retired from his job as a customs official to fulfill his dream of buying a farm. There, his already present alcoholism took more of a grip and made the household so intolerable, Adolf's teenage older brother ran away, after which more abuse centered on Adolf as Alois insisted he become a customs official. Adolf, who did not have the grades to do that, then clashed with his father insisting he be an artist instead. Then, [music] when Adolf was 13, Alois died of a stroke while drinking his morning glass of wine, leaving a traumatized son who believed strength lay in control and a hard sense of duty to the state.

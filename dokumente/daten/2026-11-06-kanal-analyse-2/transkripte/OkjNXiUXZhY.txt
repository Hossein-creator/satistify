This is how tea was produced back in the 1600s. This would be called a brick of tea or a tea cake. In China to the UK, every single bit of space was money. So you could put far more of these bricks into a ship than if you had loose bags of tea. In China and India, these were also cut into squares and the squares could be exchanged as currency and people would expect and accept a block of tea for goods and you would scrape your tea into the pot or you would have a grater and greater into your pot and because it was so expensive once you had made your tea you would then rock it back in your tea caddy and this could last up to a year.

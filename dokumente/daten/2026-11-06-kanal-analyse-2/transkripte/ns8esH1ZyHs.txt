Recently deleted history. When America entered World War II, it did so with a secret weapon, Native Americans who spoke a language unintelligible by the Axis powers. Starting in 1942, the government trained these code talkers to use radio equipment in order to create an unbreakable combat code which could be used quickly on the battlefield. Comanche code talkers supported the D-Day landings at Utah Beach. Eight members of the Meskwaki tribe served as Fox language code talkers in North Africa, but the most famous were the Navajo code talkers who served in the Marine Corps during every major battle from Guadalcanal to Iwo Jima. The program remained secret until 1968, and today code talkers have been universally hailed as heroes for decades, including in the first term of Donald Trump where he honored several during a visit to the White House. But, in March of 2025, photos and articles featuring them were removed from government websites as part of the Trump administration's purge of so-called DEI material.
